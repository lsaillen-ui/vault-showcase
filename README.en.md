## Why Vault?

Vouchers and gift cards are easy to forget. They remain buried in an email, an application or a wallet until the day their owner realizes they have expired.

Vault brings them together in one interface and helps users retrieve them at the right time.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/vault-home.png" alt="Vault home screen displaying fictional vouchers and gift cards" width="340">
</p>

## Add a voucher without friction

The experience is designed to save a voucher as soon as it is received: through manual entry, by scanning a code or from another application’s share menu.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/vault-vision.png" alt="Overview of the problem addressed by Vault and the different ways to add a voucher" width="900">
</p>

## Product vision

- Bring discount vouchers, coupons and gift cards together.
- Organize them visually by category.
- Provide a reminder before an expiration date.
- Retrieve useful information in a few seconds.
- Keep vouchers available across devices.

## Designed for everyday use

Vault aims to make one action effortless: save a voucher as soon as it is received, then forget about it until it becomes useful.

Navigation stays direct, cards remain readable and alerts can be understood at a glance.
